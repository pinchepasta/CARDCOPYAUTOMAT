<h2>An awesome Android tool for filmmakers & photographers!</h2>
It reads all kinds of raw and cine dng or .dng files, but it's also capable of transfering .mlv files like those in magic lantern.
Put in a card, press copy - and it just copies all files to your internal drive, or a google drive, then it deletes the images on the card and ejects it.
Now you have your images, backups of them, and a clean card so you can keep shooting, no time lost.
<br><br>


<br>
<br>
<br>
<b>How does it work?</b>


All you need is a usb card reader device and an android phone or tablet. You can also use a dock to add your files straight to an ssd drive instead of the phone's internal storage.
